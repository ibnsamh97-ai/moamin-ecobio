# Google Play – Store listing (English translation)

## App name
EcoBio AI – Tigris Pollution

## Short description
Real daily air & river data for Salah Al-Din districts, with AI forecasts

## Full description
EcoBio AI is a scientific platform from the Department of Biology, College of Education for Pure Sciences, Tikrit University (Iraq), for monitoring, understanding and treating environmental pollution in Salah Al-Din Governorate and along the Tigris River.

A real database, updated every day
For each of the six districts on the Tigris (Al-Shirqat, Baiji, Tikrit, Al-Dour, Samarra, Balad), a daily record from open scientific sources:
• Air pollutants (PM2.5, PM10, dust, NO₂, SO₂, ozone, CO) from Copernicus CAMS since 2022
• Weather from ERA5 since 2015
• Tigris discharge from GloFAS since 2000
• Water turbidity and chlorophyll indices from Sentinel-2 since 2017
• Pollution sources mapped in OpenStreetMap, and published Tikrit University studies
The whole database works on your phone, even offline.

Transparent AI
• Next-day and two-day PM2.5 forecasts for every district, comparing five models (random forest, gradient boosting, Ridge, neural network and a persistence baseline) on unseen data, with each model's accuracy and error range
• Anomalous-day detection with Isolation Forest
• Daily air-pattern classification (dust, summer ozone, cleaner air…) with K-Means
• Feature importance and predicted-vs-actual charts

Tools for researchers and students
• Water quality lab: WQI, HPI and compliance with Iraqi standard 417
• Environmental data analyser: upload Excel and get ANOVA, Duncan's test, correlations and charts
• Treatment plant simulator and scientific calculators
• Live SQL queries on the database, Excel and CSV export
• Optional daily air forecast notification for your district

Privacy
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location is used on your device only, to pick the nearest district.

Scientific note
Air values come from the CAMS model (~45 km), discharge from the GloFAS model, and water indices are relative satellite indicators. The app is for education, research and awareness and does not replace official monitoring stations.
